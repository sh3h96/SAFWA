# 2. Logo & Brand Identity Guidelines

---

# 2.1 Logo Overview

The SAFWA logo is the visual embodiment of the platform's mission:

"Transforming traditional automotive workshop management into an intelligent, connected, and efficient digital ecosystem."

The logo combines engineering precision with digital workflow through a single integrated symbol.

It is intentionally minimal, scalable, and technology-focused.

The identity is designed to feel equally appropriate on:

• SaaS Dashboard
• Web Application
• Mobile App
• Workshop Signage
• Business Documents
• Reports
• Marketing Materials

---

# 2.2 Logo Philosophy

Unlike traditional automotive logos that rely on literal illustrations such as cars, gears, or tools, SAFWA uses abstraction.

The logo tells a story.

First Impression:

An elegant geometric "S".

Second Impression:

A flowing intelligent process.

Third Impression:

An integrated workshop maintenance system.

The logo rewards attention without sacrificing simplicity.

Users spend most of their time reading:

• Customer information
• Vehicle details
• Work Orders
• Reports
• Inventory
• Tables
• Invoices

Typography must therefore prioritize:

• Readability
• Clarity
• Consistency
• Information hierarchy

Forms are the primary interaction point between users and the SAFWA platform.

Therefore, forms must prioritize:

• Speed
